# How to Become an AI Engineer in 2026: A Self-Study Roadmap

- Type: article

A KDnuggets article proposing a self-study path that starts with Python and basic LLM vocabulary before building small end-to-end systems, covering ML fundamentals, LLMs, prompt engineering, RAG, agentic AI, and MLOps. States the roadmap can be covered in 9-12 months of consistent study and argues a portfolio of deployed projects outweighs formal credentials for most hiring decisions.
