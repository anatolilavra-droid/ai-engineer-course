An interactive, community-maintained step-by-step roadmap defining AI Engineering as designing and implementing systems using pre-trained models and existing AI tools rather than building models from scratch. Covers topics such as prompt engineering, AI agents, AI vs AGI, common terminology, using pre-trained models, popular AI model providers (OpenAI, Gemini, Azure AI, AWS SageMaker, Hugging Face, Mistral AI, Cohere), and AI safety and ethics, plus a linked companion projects page.
