A report from DeepLearning.AI's "The Batch" newsletter presenting a map of AI engineering skills intended to help developers prioritize learning and help employers evaluate candidates. States that AI engineering skills (including working with coding agents) will be needed not just by AI engineers but also by full-stack, data, DevOps, and ML engineers, and connects the skills map to DeepLearning.AI's AI Engineer Specialization on Coursera.
