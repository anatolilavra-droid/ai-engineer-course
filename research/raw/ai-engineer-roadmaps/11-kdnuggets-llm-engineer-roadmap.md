A KDnuggets article specifically on the LLM Engineer path, structured around five skill areas: foundations, prompting and tool calling, retrieval, fine-tuning and alignment, and serving/operations. Reports demand for the role grew over 135% in 2026 as LLM features moved from internal demos into production, with senior specialist salaries cited between $220,000 and $350,000.
