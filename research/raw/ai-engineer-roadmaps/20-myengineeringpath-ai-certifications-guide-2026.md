A certification-focused guide comparing AWS, Google Cloud, and Azure AI/generative-AI certification paths relevant to AI/GenAI engineers in 2026, including AWS Certified Generative AI Developer - Professional (built around Amazon Bedrock, $300, 180-minute exam), AWS Certified Machine Learning Engineer - Associate (MLA-C01, $150, covering SageMaker training/deployment and Bedrock RAG/agent patterns), and Google Professional Machine Learning Engineer ($200, updated as of March 2026 to include Gemini models and advanced prompt engineering). Recommends choosing the certification tied to the cloud platform actually used and pairing it with a hands-on project.
