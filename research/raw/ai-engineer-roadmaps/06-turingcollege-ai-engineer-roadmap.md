# AI Engineer Roadmap: How to Become an AI Engineer in 2026

- URL: https://www.turingcollege.com/blog/ai-engineer-roadmap-how-to-become-an-ai-engineer
- Type: blog
- Published: unknown
- Topic: ai-engineer-roadmaps

A bootcamp-provider blog post describing a 12-month timeline for becoming an AI engineer: months 1-2 on Python and math foundations with small data-analysis projects, months 3-4 strengthening coding habits, months 5-6 core machine learning, months 7-8 deep learning, months 9-10 generative models, and months 11-12 specialization plus a focused capstone project. Also cites current AI engineer salary ranges of roughly $130K-$250K+.
