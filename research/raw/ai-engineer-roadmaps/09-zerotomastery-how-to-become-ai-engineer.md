- Type: blog

A Zero To Mastery blog post describing AI engineers as builders of chatbots, RAG pipelines, autonomous agents, and intelligent workflows on top of existing models, stating a PhD or advanced math background is not required to enter the field. Recommends steps of learning core programming/data/ML/software-engineering skills, building a portfolio, and applying for roles, and cites a median U.S. AI engineer salary of about $142K with entry ($90K-$135K), mid ($140K-$210K), and senior ($220K+) tiers.
