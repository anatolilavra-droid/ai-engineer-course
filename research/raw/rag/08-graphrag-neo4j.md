# What is GraphRAG? - Neo4j Graph Intelligence Platform

- URL: https://neo4j.com/blog/genai/what-is-graphrag/
- Type: blog
- Published: unknown
- Topic: rag

Explains GraphRAG as an approach that constructs a knowledge graph from source documents, extracting entities and relationships, then uses graph traversal instead of (or alongside) flat vector search for retrieval. Describes Microsoft's GraphRAG implementation, including community detection over the graph and generation of domain-specific summaries for clusters of related entities.
