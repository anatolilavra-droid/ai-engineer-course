# RAG Evaluation Metrics in 2026: Faithfulness & More

- URL: https://futureagi.com/blog/rag-evaluation-metrics-2025/
- Type: blog
- Published: 2026
- Topic: rag

Identifies six core metrics anchoring a 2026 RAG evaluation program: faithfulness, context precision, recall, groundedness, answer relevance, and hallucination rate. Explains faithfulness as the fraction of generated claims supported by retrieved context, and recommends component-wise evaluation covering both retrieval and generation stages.
