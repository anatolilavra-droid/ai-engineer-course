# RAG vs. long-context LLMs: A side-by-side comparison

- URL: https://www.meilisearch.com/blog/rag-vs-long-context-llms
- Type: blog
- Published: 2026
- Topic: rag

Compares RAG and long-context LLM approaches, noting context windows reaching roughly 1M tokens (Claude Sonnet), 2M tokens (Gemini 3 Pro), and 10M tokens (Llama 4 Scout) as of 2026. Concludes that RAG tends to be stronger on accuracy and efficiency while long-context approaches are stronger on reasoning and context retention, and that neither approach is universally superior across task types.
