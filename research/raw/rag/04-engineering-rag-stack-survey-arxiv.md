# Engineering the RAG Stack: A Comprehensive Review of the Architecture and Trust Frameworks for Retrieval-Augmented Generation Systems

- URL: https://arxiv.org/pdf/2601.05264
- Type: paper
- Published: 2026-01
- Topic: rag

An arXiv survey reviewing RAG system architecture and trust/robustness frameworks, including analysis of failure modes such as extraction errors, context size limitations, and reasoning failures in conventional RAG pipelines. Also touches on GraphRAG as part of the broader architectural landscape.
