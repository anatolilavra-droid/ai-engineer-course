# LongPIBench: A Long-Context Benchmark for Prompt Injection

- URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.28411v1
- Type: paper
- Published: 2026-08
- Topic: prompt-injection

Introduces a benchmark for prompt injection under long-context settings, covering scenarios such as paper peer review, resume screening, code review, and email summarization with context lengths from thousands to tens of thousands of tokens. Reports that current defenses are frequently bypassed by simple heuristic attacks in long-context conditions.
