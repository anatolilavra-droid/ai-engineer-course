# Context Window Optimization: 6 LLM Strategies for 2026

- URL: https://neuraltrust.ai/blog/context-window-optimization
- Type: article
- Published: 2026
- Topic: llm-basics-tokenization

Covers strategies for controlling which tokens enter an LLM's context window per request, including the "lost-in-the-middle" effect where models recall information at the beginning and end of long context better than content buried in the middle. Discusses cost/quality tradeoffs, noting usable high-accuracy context often sits at roughly 60-70% of a model's advertised maximum, and reduction techniques like sliding windows, summarization, and RAG-based retrieval.
