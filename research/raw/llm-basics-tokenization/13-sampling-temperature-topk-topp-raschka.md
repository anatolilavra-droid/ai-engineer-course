# How do temperature, top-k, and top-p sampling differ?

- URL: https://sebastianraschka.com/faq/docs/temperature-topk-topp-sampling.html
- Type: article
- Published: unknown
- Topic: llm-basics-tokenization

Explains the differences between temperature, top-k, and top-p (nucleus) sampling as decoding strategies for LLM text generation. Describes how temperature reshapes the probability distribution over the next token while top-k and top-p restrict the candidate token pool in different ways.
