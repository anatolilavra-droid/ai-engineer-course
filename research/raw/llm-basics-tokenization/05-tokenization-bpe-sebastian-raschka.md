# Tokenization and byte pair encoding

- URL: https://sebastianraschka.com/faq/docs/tokenization-bpe.html
- Type: article
- Published: unknown
- Topic: llm-basics-tokenization

A FAQ-style explainer by Sebastian Raschka covering how byte pair encoding works as a subword tokenization algorithm, starting from individual characters/bytes and merging the most frequent adjacent pairs until a target vocabulary size is reached. Explains why BPE gives broad text coverage without one vocabulary entry per possible word.
