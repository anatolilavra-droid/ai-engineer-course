# Inside the LLM Word Factory

- URL: https://arxiv.org/pdf/2606.08562
- Type: paper
- Published: 2026
- Topic: llm-basics-tokenization

Surveys emerging tokenization approaches beyond standard BPE, including tokenizer-free methods (e.g. T-FREE using sparse hashed character-trigram embeddings, MambaByte for byte-level state-space models) and dynamic/adaptive tokenization techniques such as ADAT and retrofitted dynamic tokenization. Discusses tradeoffs like vocabulary compression and inference FLOP reduction versus current BPE/SentencePiece dominance.
