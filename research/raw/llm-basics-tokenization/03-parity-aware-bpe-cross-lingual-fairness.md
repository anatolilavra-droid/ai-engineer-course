# Parity-Aware Byte-Pair Encoding: Improving Cross-lingual Fairness in Tokenization

- URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2508.04796
- Type: paper
- Published: 2025-08
- Topic: llm-basics-tokenization

Presents a modified BPE training procedure ("Parity-Aware BPE") aimed at reducing tokenization inequality across languages, reporting up to an 89% reduction in cross-lingual tokenization disparity relative to classical BPE. Reports the method has negligible impact on overall compression rate and no observed systematic degradation in downstream language-model performance.
