# LLM Sampling Parameters Explained: Intuition to Math

- URL: https://letsdatascience.com/blog/llm-sampling-temperature-top-k-top-p-and-min-p-explained
- Type: article
- Published: unknown
- Topic: llm-basics-tokenization

Covers LLM decoding/sampling parameters including temperature, top-k, top-p, and min-p, moving from intuitive explanations to the underlying math of how each reshapes or truncates the next-token probability distribution. Discusses how low temperature produces focused/repetitive output while high temperature flattens the distribution toward more random output.
