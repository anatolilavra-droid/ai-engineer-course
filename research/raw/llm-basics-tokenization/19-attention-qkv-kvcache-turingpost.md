# AI 101: Your Ultimate Guide to Attention: Mechanism, QKV, and KV Cache

- URL: https://www.turingpost.com/p/your-ultimate-guide-to-attention-mechanism-qkv-and-kv-cache
- Type: article
- Published: unknown
- Topic: llm-basics-tokenization

An introductory guide covering the attention mechanism in transformers, including how queries, keys, and values (QKV) are computed per token and how the KV cache stores past key/value tensors to avoid recomputation during autoregressive decoding. Also references Grouped Query Attention (GQA) as used in models like Llama 3 to balance cache savings with model quality.
