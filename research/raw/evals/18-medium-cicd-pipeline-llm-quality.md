# Your CI/CD Pipeline Is Your Last Line of Defence for LLM Quality

- URL: https://medium.com/@srinib100/your-ci-cd-pipeline-is-your-last-line-of-defence-for-llm-quality-3fef6a86208b
- Type: blog
- Published: 2026-04
- Topic: evals

Argues for wiring evaluation into CI/CD as a hard gate and then running the same evaluation suite continuously in production, distinguishing pre-release evaluation from ongoing production monitoring.
Describes an evaluation maturity model ranging from Level 0 (manual testing) to Level 4 (continuous eval on production traffic, automated red-teaming, and eval-driven prompt optimization), stating most teams in 2026 sit at Level 0-1.
