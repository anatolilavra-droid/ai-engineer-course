# Illusions of the Gold Standard: A Large-scale Analysis of Human Evaluation Protocols for Long-form Text Generation

- URL: https://arxiv.org/pdf/2606.07936
- Type: paper
- Published: 2026-06
- Topic: evals

An arXiv paper analyzing human evaluation protocols used for long-form text generation at scale, examining how protocol design choices affect the reliability of human judgments treated as a "gold standard."
Reports on inter-annotator variability, noting that Cohen's kappa between human annotator pairs commonly falls in the 0.5-0.8 range depending on task difficulty, even with clear guidelines.
