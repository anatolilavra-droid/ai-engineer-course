# LLM-as-a-Judge in 2026: Top Evaluation Techniques and Best Practices - DeepEval

- URL: https://deepeval.com/blog/llm-as-a-judge
- Type: blog
- Published: unknown
- Topic: evals

Explains the LLM-as-a-judge pattern where one LLM scores another's outputs against defined criteria such as answer relevancy, faithfulness, helpfulness, bias, and correctness.
Covers known judge biases (position bias, verbosity preference) and mitigation techniques including answer rotation, cross-validation, chain-of-thought prompting, and few-shot examples to improve scoring reliability.
