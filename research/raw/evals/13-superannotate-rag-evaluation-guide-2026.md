# RAG Evaluation: Complete Guide 2026 - SuperAnnotate

- URL: https://www.superannotate.com/blog/rag-evaluation
- Type: blog
- Published: unknown
- Topic: evals

Covers evaluating RAG systems across two dimensions, retrieval quality and generation quality, using metrics such as faithfulness, context precision, context recall, groundedness, answer relevance, and hallucination rate implemented by tools like RAGAS, TruLens, and DeepEval.
Notes that traditional NLP metrics (BLEU, ROUGE, BERTScore) measure surface similarity rather than whether a model faithfully used retrieved context, and that a RAG system can score well on ROUGE while ignoring its retrieved documents.
