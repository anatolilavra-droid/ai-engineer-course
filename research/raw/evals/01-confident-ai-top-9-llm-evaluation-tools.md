# Top 9 LLM Evaluation Tools in 2026 - Confident AI

- URL: https://www.confident-ai.com/knowledge-base/compare/best-llm-evaluation-tools
- Type: article
- Published: unknown
- Topic: evals

Compares nine LLM evaluation tools available in 2026, describing what a workable eval pipeline needs: a curated 100-500 example golden dataset, 3-5 task-specific metrics, an LLM-as-judge scoring layer with human spot checks, and a CI hook that blocks regressions above a defined threshold.
Notes that LLM evaluation splits into distinct sub-problems (RAG pipelines, multi-turn chatbots, AI agents) that fail in different ways and that most tools only address one of these categories.
