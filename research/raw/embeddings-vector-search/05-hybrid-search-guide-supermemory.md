- URL: https://supermemory.ai/blog/hybrid-search-guide/
- Type: blog
- Published: 2026-04

Describes hybrid search architectures that combine sparse lexical retrieval (e.g. BM25/full-text) with dense vector retrieval into a single ranked result set. Covers reported recall differences between hybrid, dense-only, and sparse-only approaches, including benchmark figures such as NDCG comparisons on the WANDS e-commerce dataset.
