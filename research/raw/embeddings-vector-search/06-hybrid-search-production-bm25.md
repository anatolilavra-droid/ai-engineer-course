# Hybrid Search in Production: Why BM25 Still Wins on the Queries That Matter

- URL: https://tianpan.co/blog/2026-04-12-hybrid-search-production-bm25-dense-embeddings
- Type: blog
- Published: 2026-04-12
- Topic: embeddings-vector-search

Discusses production hybrid search implementation combining BM25 sparse retrieval with dense embedding search, focusing on why BM25 continues to recover queries with specific names, dates, or technical terms that dense embedding models miss. Covers latency implications of running two retrievers in parallel and lists systems that support hybrid queries natively (Qdrant, Elasticsearch, OpenSearch, Weaviate, Milvus).
