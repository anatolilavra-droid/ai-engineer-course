# Matryoshka Vector Embeddings: Flexible Embeddings for Cost-Efficient AI Systems

- URL: https://vast.ai/article/matryoshka-vector-embeddings
- Type: blog
- Published: 2026
- Topic: embeddings-vector-search

Explains Matryoshka Representation Learning (MRL), a training technique that orders the most important information in the earliest embedding dimensions so that embeddings can be truncated to smaller sizes with limited performance loss. Covers reported storage and performance trade-offs at different truncated dimension sizes.
