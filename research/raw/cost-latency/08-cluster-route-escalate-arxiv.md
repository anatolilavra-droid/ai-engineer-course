- URL: https://arxiv.org/html/2606.27457
- Type: paper
- Published: 2026-06
- Topic: cost-latency

Academic paper proposing a cascaded framework that clusters queries, routes them, and escalates to larger models only when needed, aimed at cost-aware LLM serving. Part of a research line studying dynamic model routing and cascading for efficient inference.
