# LLM Cost Optimization Strategies: Complete Tactics List [2026]

- URL: https://atlan.com/know/ai-agent/llm-cost-optimization-strategies/
- Type: article
- Published: 2026
- Topic: cost-latency

Describes a six-step optimization approach: auditing agent/automation inventory for dead or duplicate workloads, then applying semantic and prompt caching, model routing and tiering, prompt compression, batching, and quantization or distillation for self-hosted models. States that output tokens are the biggest cost driver since their pricing is typically 2-5x that of input tokens.
