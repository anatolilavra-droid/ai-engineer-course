# How speculative decoding delivers faster LLM inference

- URL: https://developers.redhat.com/articles/2026/06/12/how-speculative-decoding-delivers-faster-llm-inference
- Type: article
- Published: 2026-06-12
- Topic: cost-latency

Explains the mechanics of speculative decoding for production inference serving and references Medusa's parallel token-head/tree-based-attention approach achieving up to 3.6x decoding acceleration. Also cites AMD MI300X benchmarks showing 3.6x combined improvement from FP8 quantization plus speculative decoding on Llama 3.1-405B.
