# Chain of Thought Prompting in 2026: Guide for GPT-5 + Claude 4.7

- URL: https://futureagi.com/blog/chain-of-thought-prompting-ai-2025/
- Type: blog
- Published: 2025
- Topic: prompt-engineering

Guide comparing chain-of-thought prompting behavior across GPT-5's numeric reasoning-effort dial (1-5) and Claude Opus's adaptive reasoning that auto-calibrates thinking depth by query complexity. Argues explicit CoT prompting remains relevant even with reasoning-native models because it offers a level of control over accuracy and auditability that native reasoning alone does not provide, and mentions newer variants like Trace-of-Thought.
