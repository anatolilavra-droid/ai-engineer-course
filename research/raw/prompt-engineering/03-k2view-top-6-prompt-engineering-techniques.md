# Prompt engineering techniques: Top 6 for 2026

- URL: https://www.k2view.com/blog/prompt-engineering-techniques/
- Type: blog
- Published: 2026
- Topic: prompt-engineering

A vendor blog post enumerating six prompting techniques positioned as most relevant for 2026, including chain-of-thought, tool-augmented prompting, retrieval grounding, and self-reflection prompts, noting these are becoming standard practice in enterprise AI systems. Also covers meta-prompting as a technique that structures responses using abstract format/logic guidance rather than detailed examples.
