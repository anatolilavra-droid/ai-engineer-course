# Prompt Engineering: Advanced Techniques for 2026

- URL: https://www.digitalapplied.com/blog/prompt-engineering-advanced-techniques-2026
- Type: blog
- Published: 2026
- Topic: prompt-engineering

Blog post covering advanced 2026 prompting approaches such as Chain-of-Symbol (CoS) prompting for spatial/structured-planning tasks, the CTF (Context-Task-Format) prompt segmentation method, and a shift away from temperature tuning toward setting a "reasoning_effort" parameter (Low/Medium/High) for reasoning models. Also describes "adaptive prompting," where systems incorporate real-time feedback to self-optimize prompts.
