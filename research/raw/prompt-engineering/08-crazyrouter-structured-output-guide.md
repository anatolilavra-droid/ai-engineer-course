Comparative guide to structured-output mechanisms across providers: OpenAI's response_format json_object/json_schema, Google Gemini's response_schema parameter, and Anthropic Claude's tool-use-based approach to constrained output. Describes a shift toward "schema-first development" (defining schemas in Zod or Pydantic before writing prompts) and reports reliability figures for native JSON mode, schema-first prompting, grammar-based decoding, and function/tool calling.
