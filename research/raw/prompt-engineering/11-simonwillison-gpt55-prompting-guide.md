# GPT-5.5 prompting guide

- Type: blog
- Published: 2026-04-25
- Topic: prompt-engineering

Simon Willison's write-up on OpenAI's official GPT-5.5 prompting guide, summarizing its recommendation to write "outcome-first" prompts that define the desired outcome, constraints, evidence requirements, and final answer shape while leaving the model room to choose its own path. Highlights guidance to send a short user-visible acknowledgment before long multi-step tool-calling sequences.
